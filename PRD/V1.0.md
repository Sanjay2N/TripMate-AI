# Product Requirements Document (PRD)  
**Product Name:** AI Travel Agent (Working Title: "TripMate AI")  
**Version:** 1.0

**Date:** October 2026  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

TripMate AI is an AI-powered personal travel agent that plans, refines, and supports trips end-to-end like a human travel agent. It starts as a smart suggestion engine and evolves into an in-trip companion that adapts to real-time changes, explains places (including from photos), and helps users maximize their travel experience within their constraints.

The product targets tech-savvy Indian travelers (domestic and international) who want personalized, stress-free trip planning and on-trip support without relying on multiple apps or manual research. [web:2][web:4][web:10]

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Travelers face:

- **Overwhelming planning effort**: Researching destinations, places, routes, weather, documents, and budgets across multiple sources is time-consuming and fragmented. [web:8][web:13]
- **Generic itineraries**: Most tools provide static, one-size-fits-all plans that don't reflect personal preferences, pace, or constraints. [web:2][web:10]
- **No in-trip support**: Once the trip starts, users are on their own when plans go wrong (delays, closures, weather, fatigue). [web:4][web:15]
- **Lack of personalization over time**: Apps don't learn from past trips to improve future recommendations. [web:2][web:13]

TripMate AI addresses these by acting as a single, intelligent, conversational travel agent that plans, explains, adapts, and learns.

---

## 3. Goals & Success Metrics

### 3.1 Product Goals

1. **Reduce planning time** by at least 50% compared to manual research. [web:13]
2. **Increase trip satisfaction** by delivering personalized, constraint-aware itineraries. [web:2][web:4]
3. **Provide human-like in-trip support**, reducing stress during disruptions. [web:4][web:15]
4. **Build a learning travel profile** that improves recommendations over multiple trips. [web:2][web:13]

### 3.2 Key Metrics (Non-Technical)

- **Planning Efficiency**
  - Average time from "start planning" to "finalized itinerary"
  - Number of iterations (changes) before finalizing

- **Quality & Satisfaction**
  - User rating of itineraries (1–5): "How well does this plan match your preferences?"
  - % of users who say they "would use this for their next trip"
  - Self-reported stress level before vs during trip

- **Engagement**
  - Number of in-trip interactions per day
  - % of users who use photo-based explanations or real-time adjustments

- **Retention**
  - % of users who plan more than one trip within 6 months
  - % of users who share the tool with friends/family

## 4. Target Users & Personas

### 4.1 Primary Persona: "The Planner" (Core Target)

- **Profile**: 28–40, urban professional, Bengaluru-based, tech-savvy
- **Behavior**: Likes detailed planning, optimizes for experience and budget, travels with family or friends 2–4 times/year
- **Needs**:
  - Fast, personalized itineraries
  - Clear trade-offs and budget breakdowns
  - On-trip help when things go wrong

### 4.2 Secondary Persona: "The Spontaneous Traveler"

- **Profile**: 22–35, solo or with friends, prefers flexible plans
- **Behavior**: Books late, likes recommendations, okay with rough plans
- **Needs**:
  - Quick "best of" suggestions
  - Real-time ideas on the go
  - Simple, low-effort interface

### 4.3 Tertiary Persona: "The Family Traveler"

- **Profile**: 30–45, traveling with spouse + kids or parents
- **Behavior**: Prioritizes comfort, safety, kid-friendly activities
- **Needs**:
  - Family-aware itineraries (nap times, stroller access, etc.)
  - Safety and health information
  - Easy coordination with other family members

## 5. User Stories & Use Cases

Organized by phase.

### Phase 1 — Smart Suggestion Engine (MVP)

**US1.1 – Create a Trip Plan from Scratch**  
*As a* traveler,  
*I want to* enter my source, destination, days, budget, and preferences,  
*So that* I get a personalized day-by-day itinerary with places, timings, and notes.  

**US1.2 – See Multiple Plan Variants**  
*As a* budget-conscious traveler,  
*I want to* see Budget / Balanced / Premium versions of my plan,  
*So that* I can choose based on my comfort and spending limits.  

**US1.3 – Understand Trade-offs**  
*As a* planner,  
*I want to* see what I gain/lose if I drop or add an activity,  
*So that* I can make informed decisions about my day.  

**US1.4 – Get Pre-Trip Guidance**  
*As a* first-time visitor to a destination,  
*I want* weather, document, packing, and safety tips,  
*So that* I am prepared and avoid surprises.  

---

### Phase 2 — Interactive Planning & Personalization

**US2.1 – Refine Plan via Chat**  
*As a* user,  
*I want to* say "make Day 2 more relaxed" or "add adventure activities,"  
*So that* the itinerary updates without me rebuilding it manually.  

**US2.2 – Save & Reuse Preferences**  
*As a* frequent traveler,  
*I want* my preferences (pace, interests, dietary, comfort) to be remembered,  
*So that* future trips are faster and more relevant.  

**US2.3 – Simulate What-If Scenarios**  
*As a* planner,  
*I want to* adjust budget, days, or pace and see the plan change instantly,  
*So that* I can explore options before committing.  

**US2.4 – Plan Multi-City Trips**  
*As a* traveler,  
*I want to* plan trips with multiple destinations (e.g., Goa + Gokarna),  
*So that* I can handle complex itineraries in one place.  

---

### Phase 3 — Booking & Pre-Trip Operations

**US3.1 – View Booking Options from Itinerary**  
*As a* user,  
*I want to* see suggested flights/trains/hotels/activities from my plan,  
*So that* I can move from planning to booking seamlessly.  

**US3.2 – Get Policy-Aware Recommendations**  
*As a* risk-averse traveler,  
*I want* options that match my constraints (refundable, no red-eye, family rooms),  
*So that* I don't compromise on my priorities.  

**US3.3 – Receive Price Alerts**  
*As a* flexible planner,  
*I want to* be notified when prices drop for my preferred dates,  
*So that* I can book at a better price.  

**US3.4 – Coordinate with Group**  
*As a* group traveler,  
*I want to* share the itinerary and assign tasks (who books what),  
*So that* planning is distributed and clear.

**US4.1 – Adjust Plan in Real Time**  
*As a* traveler on the trip,  
*I want to* say "I'm 2 hours late" or "I'm too tired,"  
*So that* the plan is re-optimized for today.  

**US4.2 – Get Explanations from Photos**  
*As a* curious traveler,

**US4.3 – Handle Disruptions**  
*As a* traveler facing a delay or closure,  
*I want* alternative plans and options,  
*So that* my trip isn't ruined by unexpected events.  

**US4.4 – Get On-Ground Logistics Help**  
*As a* visitor in a new city,  
*I want* last-mile guidance (metro/bus/fare) and crowd tips,  
*So that* I can move around confidently.  

**US4.5 – Access Safety & Emergency Info**  
*As a* traveler,  
*I want* emergency numbers, nearby hospitals, and safety tips,  
*So that* I feel secure during the trip.  

**US4.6 – Track Expenses vs Budget**  
*As a* budget-conscious traveler,  
*I want to* log expenses and see remaining budget,  
*So that* I don't overspend unexpectedly.

## 6. Functional Requirements (Non-Technical)

### 6.1 Onboarding & Profile

- Users can create an account and set basic preferences (pace, interests, budget range, dietary, comfort).
- Users can optionally describe past trips or preferences in free text.
- System stores a "Travel Profile" that is used for all future plans.

### 6.2 Trip Creation & Inputs

- Users can create a new trip with:
  - Source, destination(s), duration (days/nights)
  - Travel mode(s): bus/train/air/car/rental
  - Budget (total and/or per day)
  - Party type: solo/couple/family (with kids' ages)/friends
  - Preferences: pace, interests, comfort, dietary, risk tolerance
- System allows partial inputs and fills sensible defaults.

### 6.3 Itinerary Generation

- System generates:
  - Day-by-day plan with time slots, places, travel times, and notes
  - At least 2–3 variants (Budget/Balanced/Premium)
  - Budget breakdown (transport, stay, activities, food estimate)
  - Experience score (how well it matches preferences)
- Each place includes:
  - Why visit, suggested duration, entry fee range, best time to visit
  - Opening hours and any known closures (if available)

### 6.4 Trade-offs & Explainability

- For each day, system shows:
  - What can be dropped to save time
  - What can be added if user has extra time/energy
  - Why certain areas/hotels were chosen (proximity to activities)
- System explains recommendations in plain language.

### 6.5 Contextual Guidance (Pre-Trip)

- For each destination & date range, system provides:
  - Weather summary and best times to visit key places
  - Document checklist (ID, permits, visa, insurance)
  - Packing suggestions (clothing, footwear, essentials)
  - Safety & awareness tips (scams, dress codes, local customs)
  - Local emergency numbers and key contacts

### 6.6 Conversational Refinement

- Users can chat with the system to:
  - Change pace ("make Day 3 relaxed")
  - Add/remove interests ("add one adventure activity")
  - Replace types of places ("no temples, suggest alternatives")
- System updates the itinerary in place and shows what changed.

### 6.7 What-If Simulator

- Users can adjust:
  - Budget (±%)
  - Duration (±1–2 days)
  - Pace (more relaxed ↔ more packed)
- System instantly shows updated itinerary and budget.

### 6.8 Multi-City & Route Planning

- Users can add multiple destinations in one trip.
- System plans:
  - Inter-city travel options (train/bus/flight suggestions)
  - Per-city day plans
  - Logical route order to minimize backtracking

### 6.9 Booking Module

- From finalized itinerary, users can:
  - View suggested flights/trains/hotels/activities
  - Filter by policies (refundable, family rooms, no red-eye)
  - See total cost and breakdown
  - "Confirm booking" (mock or real) and receive confirmation summary
- System explains why each option was chosen.

### 6.10 Price Alerts & Group Coordination

- Users can mark trips as "planning, not booked."
- System monitors prices (or simulates) and sends alerts for significant drops.
- Users can:
  - Share itinerary link (read-only) with family/friends
  - Add comments/notes on specific days/activities
  - Assign tasks (who books what)

### 6.11 In-Trip Companion

- On trip days, users see:
  - Today's plan with status (not started/in progress/done)
  - Ability to report delays, fatigue, or changes
- System:
  - Re-optimizes the day's plan
  - Suggests alternatives if places are closed or weather is bad
  - Provides last-mile guidance (transport, fares, crowd tips)

### 6.12 Photo-Based Explanations

- Users can upload a photo of a place/monument/object.
- System:
  - Identifies the place/object
  - Explains significance, key features, etiquette, and photo tips
  - Provides short, engaging narratives (not textbook-style)

### 6.13 Disruption Handling

- When disruptions occur (delay, closure, weather):
  - System proposes revised plans for the rest of the day/trip
  - Shows trade-offs (what's dropped, what's deferred)
  - If integrated, suggests rebooking options

### 6.14 Expense Tracking

- Users can log expenses manually or via receipt photo.
- System:
  - Categorizes spend (food, transport, stay, activities)
  - Shows remaining budget vs plan
  - Warns if overspending trend is detected

### 6.15 Safety & Duty-of-Care

- Pre-trip:
  - Emergency numbers, hospitals, police stations per city
  - Safety tips relevant to destination and activities
- On-trip:
  - Contextual alerts (severe weather, local issues)
  - Quick access to emergency info

### 6.16 Post-Trip Report

- After trip ends, system generates:
  - Timeline of activities (map + list)
  - Expense summary
  - Highlights (top places, photos if integrated)
  - "What worked / what to change" section
- This feeds into the user's travel profile for better future plans.

## 7. Non-Functional Requirements (Behavioral & Experience)

These are not technical performance specs, but experience expectations.

### 7.1 Trust & Transparency

- Recommendations must be explainable in plain language.
- System should show data freshness (e.g., "Opening hours verified 2 weeks ago") where possible. [web:10]
- Confidence indicators for suggestions (high/medium/low) when data is uncertain.

### 7.2 Proactivity

- System should not just answer questions; it should:
  - Warn about likely issues (crowds, weather, delays)
  - Suggest improvements ("You'll pass near X; want to add a quick stop?")
  - Prompt users before key decisions ("Prices are rising; want to book now?") [web:4][web:15]

### 7.3 Personalization

- Tone and recommendations should adapt to:
  - Family vs solo travel
  - Budget vs premium preferences
  - Risk tolerance (cautious vs adventurous)
- Over time, system should "feel like it knows the user." [web:2][web:13]

### 7.4 Simplicity & Clarity

- Interfaces should be clean and focused on decisions, not data dumps.
- Complex information (budget, routes, trade-offs) should be visualized simply (timelines, maps, summaries).

### 7.5 Reliability & Consistency

- Itineraries must consistently respect hard constraints (dates, budget ceiling, non-negotiable preferences).
- In-trip advice should be coherent with the original plan unless changes are explicitly made.

## 8. Out of Scope (For Initial Versions)

To keep the project interview-friendly and buildable:

- Full real-time booking integrations with all providers (can be mocked or partially integrated).
- Complex group payment splitting and settlements.
- Full-fledged social network or community features.
- AR-based navigation or advanced visual features.
- Corporate travel management features.

These can be mentioned as "future roadmap" in interviews.

## 9. Assumptions & Dependencies

- Users have basic smartphone and internet access for planning and most in-trip features.
- Some data sources (places, weather, transport) are available via APIs or public datasets. [web:4][web:8]
- For a personal project, some booking and price-monitoring features can be simulated with realistic mock data.
- Users are okay with sharing basic preferences and trip details to get personalized plans.

## 10. Risks & Mitigations (Product-Level)

- **Risk:** Over-promising on real-time data accuracy.  
  **Mitigation:** Clearly label data freshness and confidence; allow user corrections.

- **Risk:** Users don't trust AI plans enough to rely on them.  
  **Mitigation:** Provide transparent explanations, multiple variants, and easy manual overrides.

- **Risk:** In-trip features require connectivity in low-network areas.  
  **Mitigation:** Plan for offline access to critical info (itinerary, emergency contacts, maps) in later phases.

- **Risk:** Feature creep makes the project too big for a personal portfolio.  
  **Mitigation:** Strictly prioritize Phases 1–3 for MVP; treat Phases 4–5 as stretch goals.

## Appendix A: Glossary

- **Itinerary**: Day-by-day plan of activities, places, and logistics for a trip.
- **Travel Profile**: Persistent user preferences and history used to personalize recommendations.
- **What-If Simulator**: Feature that lets users adjust constraints and see updated plans instantly.
- **In-Trip Companion**: Real-time assistance during the trip, including re-planning and explanations.
